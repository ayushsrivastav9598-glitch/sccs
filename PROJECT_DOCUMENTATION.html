1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

Academic coordination across higher education institutions often suffers from fragmented room booking processes, lack of real-time vacancy visibility, and disconnected notification mechanisms. At Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gorakhpur University (DDUGU), the Institute of Engineering & Technology (IET) and nearby Deeksha Bhawan (~100 metres walking distance) accommodate six engineering disciplines across four academic years.

The Critical Incident Solved: During the NSS Minor Examination, multiple faculty members simultaneously reserved Room G-01 and LH-04 via uncoordinated paper notices and informal WhatsApp messages. Students arrived to find two concurrent examinations scheduled in the same hall. This forced an emergency relocation across the 100-meter corridor between IET and Deeksha Bhawan, causing severe delay, confusion, and missed examination sessions.

The Smart Campus Classroom Management System (SCCS) resolves these institutional bottlenecks through:

  • Mathematical Interval Overlap Engine: Blocks concurrent reservations at the database level with HTTP 409 Conflict.
  • Distance-Aware Heuristic Suggester: Automatically recommends alternative rooms across IET and Deeksha Bhawan, penalizing Deeksha Bhawan by 15 points due to the 100m walk.
  • Synchronized Digital Twin: Live 2D spatial vacancy blueprints for all three floors of IET and 14 lecture halls of Deeksha Bhawan.
  • Academic Timetable Discovery: Filterable schedules by branch and year with instant CSV spreadsheet download.
  • Student Grievance & Clash Reporting Flow: Direct channel for students to report collisions, with administrative resolution notes.
  • Immutable Security Audit Trail: Chronological mutation log tracking every booking, rescheduling, and notice publication.

3. Algorithmic Formulations

A. Temporal Interval Overlap Detection

Given an existing reservation interval $[S_1, E_1)$ and a requested booking interval $[S_2, E_2)$ on date $D$:

Conflict = (S1 < E2) AND (S2 < E1) ⇔ max(S1, S2) < min(E1, E2)

Here, $S$ and $E$ represent minutes from midnight ($00:00 = 0, 23:59 = 1439$). If this condition evaluates to true for any non-cancelled booking in the room, the atomic reservation is rejected immediately and the heuristic recommendation pipeline is triggered.

B. Distance-Aware Alternative Venue Heuristic Fit-Scoring

When an overlap or capacity overflow occurs, candidate vacant rooms $r$ are dynamically ranked using:

Fit Score(r) = max(0, 100 - C_pen - D_pen + B_floor + B_smart)

Where:
• C_pen = min(30.0, 0.4 × |Capacity(r) - Expected_Students|)
• D_pen = 15 points if room is in Deeksha Bhawan (~100m walking distance)
• B_floor = +10 points if room is on the same floor as requested
• B_smart = +15 points if projector & smart audio match requested criteria

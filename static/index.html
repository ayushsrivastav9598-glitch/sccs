Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Governance Matrix

Detailed breakdown of what each user role (Student, Faculty, System Administrator) can and cannot do across DDUGU IET & Deeksha Bhawan.

STUDENT ROLE

🎓 Student Persona

What Students CAN do:
  • View live 2D floor plans & room occupancy states.
  • View branch timetable (CSE, AI ML, ECE, ME, IT).
  • Download timetable in CSV / JSON format.
  • Read urgent minor examination & room notices.
  • File conflict / grievance reports on schedule clashes.
  • Ask AI Assistant for schedule & vacancy queries.
Restrictions (Safety Boundary):
  • Cannot book or reserve rooms.
  • Cannot reschedule or cancel official bookings.
  • Cannot alter official bulletins or audit logs.
Demo Account: student.cse@ddugu.ac.in / student123
FACULTY ROLE

👨‍🏫 Faculty Persona

What Faculty CAN do:
  • Search vacancy with capacity & equipment filters.
  • Book rooms for Extra Classes, Minor Exams, Labs & Seminars.
  • Automatic Conflict Blocker & Smart Alternative Suggestions.
  • Reschedule & cancel their OWN reservations.
  • Publish official academic notices & urgent exam alerts.
  • Review & resolve student grievance reports with remarks.
Restrictions (Departmental Integrity):
  • Cannot cancel or reschedule another faculty's booking.
  • Cannot delete immutable system audit logs.
Demo Account: prof.sharma@ddugu.ac.in / faculty123
SYSTEM ADMIN

🛡️ Administrator Persona

What System Admins CAN do:
  • Campus-wide override: cancel/reschedule ANY booking.
  • Access full chronological immutable Audit Trail.
  • Manage room capacities, permitted uses, & facilities.
  • Moderate, publish, and delete official notices.
  • Resolve or dismiss all student conflict reports.
  • Inspect registered users across all branches.
Governance Responsibility:
  • Maintains campus schedule coordination & resolves disputes.

📋 Roles & Permissions Guide

SCCS uses Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to ensure security, prevent scheduling overlaps, and avoid examination miscommunication.

🎓 Student Role
Allowed: Live 2D vacancy blueprints, branch timetable, download CSV, read exam notices, submit conflict reports.
Prohibited: Cannot book, reschedule, or cancel rooms.
👨‍🏫 Faculty Role
Allowed: Search rooms, book extra lectures / minor exams / labs, conflict blocker & alternatives, reschedule/cancel OWN bookings, post notices, resolve student reports.
Prohibited: Cannot cancel other faculties' reservations.
🛡️ System Administrator
Allowed: Campus-wide overrides, override ANY reservation, full audit trail access, manage room capacities, moderate notices, resolve grievances.
